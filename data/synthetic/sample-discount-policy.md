# Northwind SaaS — Discount Policy & Deal Desk Playbook

**Effective: 1 January 2026**

## Discount approval thresholds

| Discount | Authority required |
|---|---|
| 0–10% | Sales rep — no approval needed |
| 10–15% | First-line manager approval |
| 15–25% | Deal desk approval |
| 25–35% | VP Sales + CFO approval |
| 35%+ | CEO approval, exceptions only |

Any discount above 15% is considered **off-policy** and must be logged with
the approver's name and a written rationale in the CRM.

## Trade-for-concession rules

A discount above 15% must trade for at least one of:

1. A multi-year term (24 or 36 months).
2. Annual upfront prepayment.
3. A signed reference / case study.
4. A higher commit on the value metric (e.g. more seats or usage credits).

If none of these is included, the deal must be re-scoped, not re-priced.

## Quarter-end discipline

Quarter-end discounting beyond the standard ladder is **explicitly
prohibited**. Deals being pushed in the final two weeks of a quarter that
require an exceptional discount must be deferred to the next quarter or
escalated to the CFO.

## Segment-specific guidance

- **Enterprise**: Default to multi-year terms; discount appetite is higher
  but must trade for term length. Reference price is the published list.
- **MidMarket**: Standard 12-month terms; off-policy approvals should be
  rare. Watch for competitor presence.
- **SMB**: List price is the floor. Discounting above 10% requires a
  documented reason.

## Off-policy escalation path

1. Rep submits discount request with justification in the CRM.
2. Deal desk reviews within 24 hours.
3. If approved, deal desk records the approver name and the trade-back.
4. Monthly review: deal desk surfaces all off-policy approvals to the CFO.

## Failure modes (the things this policy exists to prevent)

- Discounts given because "it's quarter-end" with no trade-back.
- Multiple small discounts compounding to >20% on the same deal.
- Off-policy approvals with no recorded approver.
- Reference-price erosion through repeated discounting at the same accounts.
