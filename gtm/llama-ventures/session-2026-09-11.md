# What their agent actually probed

It did not ask about market size, business model, or pricing. Not once. It spent
the entire session on two things, which is worth knowing before the next one.

**1. Founder-market fit, asked three separate times.** It kept pushing for an
operator origin story: sales ops, finance, engineering at a company that lived the
pain. Pricekeel does not have one. The answer that finally landed was refusing the
premise, stating plainly that there is no scar story, and offering the real
trigger instead: the first product (a retrospective diagnostic) was built, worked,
and was unsellable because nothing in the buyer's week depended on it, so the wedge
was reselected deliberately against four criteria (weekly cadence, an existing
ritual and owner, data already in the CRM, and usefulness to a single customer with
no network). Their agent explicitly called that "evidence, not a claim."

**Lesson: it was testing whether the founder would invent a background.** Two
refusals to accept a false premise scored better than any story would have.

**2. The governance gap, asked four separate times.** It would not accept a
description and kept asking for the mechanism. What satisfied it was the concrete
failure path: one person holding the shared tenant code requests an exception as
one typed name and approves it as another, the string comparison passes, and the
record shows a four-eyes approval that never happened. Plus the consequence: the
record is good evidence of what was decided and weak evidence of who decided it.

It also twice framed the gap as something discovered in a real deployment. That
framing was corrected both times, because there is no deployment. Accepting it
would have been the single easiest overclaim in the session.

**Lesson: describe the failure path, not the feature.** And expect an intake agent
to offer flattering premises that are false.

# What was claimed, and it all held the boundary

Zero paying customers, two design-partner conversations at intro-call stage with no
NDA and no data, solo founder with no operator experience, a Developer Edition org
rather than a customer production org, the managed-package subscriber install still
open, the moat conceded as a thesis rather than an asset, and the wedge SAM
volunteered as small by construction. The ARR band was stated as $20M to $100M,
matching the deck, the live site, and the SAM derivation.

# For the next intake

session and it did not appear until the fifth message. It belongs in the opener,
