Three factual corrections, offered because your note invites them.

**1. Competitive positioning was addressed, not skipped.** The read says I did not

proves it.

**2. Moat was discussed.** I argued three structural points: neutrality, since a
billing platform that benchmarks your prices profits from higher prices and can
only see its own billed base; a decision corpus that compounds per customer; and
explainability as the category rather than a feature. I also stated the
uncomfortable half, that the moat is a thesis today and not an asset, because I
have zero customers.

records no pricing background. My degree is an M.S. in Pricing from Simon Business
School, which I mentioned several times. What I do not have, and said plainly
three times, is operator experience: I have never run a deal desk or carried a
quota. That gap is real and I am not softening it. But the domain training is the
reason the engine maps every metric to a named framework and the reason an LLM is
never allowed to produce a number in it.
