Where the change is confirmed by a dated post it is marked "change confirmed" below. For the others, glance at the page and soften to "use of" if you prefer.
4. **Variant P (2 people).** The template says "the clearest thing I have read on AI pricing this month". Read the post first so it is true. Rows 23 and 28.
5. **LinkedIn URLs.** Search results did not return first-party LinkedIn profile pages for these people. Five URLs below were listed by third-party pages (marked "3rd-party listed"); verify the profile is the right person. For everyone else search LinkedIn for name plus company.
6. No personal email, phone or home location is recorded. No Salesforce-ICP funnel rows are created from this list; log touches in `docs/ops/ai-founder-interview-log.csv` once actually sent.
