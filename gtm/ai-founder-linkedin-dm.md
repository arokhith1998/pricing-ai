# AI founder LinkedIn DM, short form

Founder instruction 2026-11-10: a short, crisp LinkedIn DM to anyone who falls into the category, 30 a day, drafted by the principles.

## Principles applied

1. Credibility first, never a pitch. The founder teaches, the reader decides. (Opportunity move 5: when prospects learn from your thinking the sale is halfway closed.)
2. One specific, public signal about them, named in the first line. No signal, no message.
3. One ask, small and reversible: a short conversation or a one-line answer. Nothing about software, pricing or a demo.
4. Plain words, no jargon, no numbers we cannot source, no claims about Pricekeel results or customers (the evidence boundary; the ten-word line is "Know your cost to serve before you choose a unit.").
5. Fits the channel. A connection note must be under 300 characters. A first DM is one screen on a phone, under 500 characters.
6. Easy no. Every message says a short reply is enough.
7. No em dashes.

## Who qualifies (from gtm/ai-founder-targets.md)

A founder, co-founder, CEO or head of product or pricing at an AI-native company whose public pricing page shows credits, metered usage, per-outcome pricing, or a recent move away from flat or per-seat plans, and where the model-provider cost is visibly passed through or metered. Score 50 or more on the targets rubric. Skip anyone already in the funnel, anyone at a company above the band without a visible pricing transition, and anyone with no public signal to cite.

## The connection note (under 300 characters, send with the invite)

Variant P, they posted about pricing:

> Hi {first}, your post on {topic} is the clearest thing I have read on AI pricing this month. I run a pricing diagnostic for B2B and AI software companies and I am collecting how founders decide what to charge per unit. Would value a short exchange.

Variant N, no post, pricing page signal only:

> Hi {first}, {company}'s move to {credits / metered usage / per-outcome pricing} caught my eye. I run a pricing diagnostic for B2B and AI software companies and I am collecting how founders decide what to charge per unit. Would value a short exchange.

Both are under 300 characters before placeholders are filled. Recount after filling.

## The DM (after they accept, or as a first message where messaging is open)

> Thanks for connecting, {first}. One question and no pitch: when {company} moved to {credits / metered usage / per-outcome pricing}, how did you work out what a heavy customer actually costs you to serve? I am interviewing fifteen founders on exactly that and sharing back what I learn, anonymised. Twenty minutes, your timing. A one-line answer here is just as useful.

Under 400 characters before placeholders. One signal, one question, one ask, an easy no.

## Follow-up, once, seven days later, only if no reply

> {first}, one line from me and then I will leave it: the pattern so far is that founders find their most expensive customer by accident, from a provider bill. If that is not your experience I would like to hear why. If it is, twenty minutes would help me and I will send you the write-up.

The "pattern so far" sentence may be used only once at least five interviews have said it. Until then use: "the question I keep asking is how founders find their most expensive customer."

## What never goes in

No price, no audit offer, no "margin" claim about their business, no Pricekeel feature, no link in the first two messages, no flattery beyond the one specific line, no deadline, no "quick call?" openers.

## Volume and mechanics

LinkedIn's own invitation limit is roughly 100 a week per account, and messaging non-connections needs InMail or an open profile. Thirty actions a day fits only as a mix: about 15 new invites plus about 15 DMs and follow-ups to people who accepted, on weekdays. There is no API for sending LinkedIn messages; Composio's LinkedIn toolkit is posting and reading only. Third-party sending tools work by automating the founder's browser session, which LinkedIn's terms forbid and which risks restriction of the only account the content strategy depends on. The queue is therefore prepared by agents and sent by the founder, from a daily tab in the research sheet: name, company, public signal with its URL and date, variant, filled text, date sent, reply. Every reply is logged in docs/ops/ai-founder-interview-log.csv.
