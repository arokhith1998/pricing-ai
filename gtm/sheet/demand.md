# Founder sheet tab: Demand

Drafted 2026-11-10 for the empty Demand tab of the "Pricekeel" sheet. One paragraph per cell in column A. Measured means counted from a named source. Estimate means derived from assumptions. Nothing here is evidence for the website.

1. What Demand We Are Testing
Hypothesis One, Pain: founders of AI-native usage or hybrid companies feel inference cost as a pricing problem, not only an engineering bill.
Hypothesis Two, Workaround: they reconcile model-provider bills against customer revenue by hand today, in a spreadsheet, notebook or script.
Hypothesis Three, Payment: they will pay 7,500 upfront for a fixed 14-day audit returning contribution margin per customer on declared cost (F1, F4).

2. Evidence We Have, and Do Not Have
Pricing Pages (measured, seen 2026-11-10, gtm/ai-founder-targets.md): 16 AI companies show credits, seat-plus-credit hybrids or model pass-through. Wording comes from model-written page summaries and is unverified.
Platform Census (measured, 2026-11-07, vault census note): 25 of 47 independent usage or hybrid accounts are AI-native. Most post RevOps or GTM-systems roles. The visible billing layer is Orb or Metronome. 3 of 50 were acquired in about 14 months.
Market Mix (secondary, via the 2026-11-06 sizing review): ICONIQ 2025 reports 19 percent usage plus 38 percent hybrid pricing. Sapphire 2026 counts 80+ AI-native companies above 100M ARR globally.
Founder Synthesis (unverified, 2026-11-10): the Consumer Trends tab is a reading of operator playbooks, not data.
Not Yet Evidence: zero interviews, zero founders naming a reconciliation artefact, zero audits offered or paid, no verified ARR for any AI-native account, no sample OpenAI or Anthropic cost export, no pricing transition confirmed by a Wayback diff.

3. Who Feels It Most
Rank One, Series B+ AI-native, 20M to 100M ARR, platform fee plus model pass-through: the ICP. Signal: a pricing page billing model or telephony cost as its own line (Vapi, Retell, Cartesia pattern) plus deal-desk or RevOps postings. Pain, a finance owner and billing history in one account.
Rank Two, Series A to B app layer, seat-to-credits hybrid: Signal: seats and credits on one page, spend caps or top-ups, a dated pricing change (Lindy, CodeRabbit, Mintlify pattern).
Rank Three, Outcome-priced agents: Signal: per-resolution charges over a platform floor (Lorikeet pattern). Cost per outcome varies, the price does not.
Rank Four, Seed, multi-provider, flat or seat pricing: Signal: founder posts on token cost, or a billing hire at a 20-person company. Most acute pain, least ability to pay or measure.
Why Seed Is a Learning Segment: tens of customers, inference bills untagged by customer, the founder as sole buyer, 35 percent assumed annual churn. CFO: 1M ARR at 30K ACV needs about 47 signups, more than the whole five-year SOM. Interview and audit them to learn, do not build for them (F3).

4. How Big, Honestly
AI-Native, 20M to 100M, US, B2B, Usage or Hybrid (estimate): about 13 to 92 accounts. From the 2026-11-06 chain (240 to 480 AI-native above 10M ARR, assumed), half assumed above 20M, minus the 80 above 100M, then US 70 to 80, B2B 75 to 85 and usage or hybrid 60 to 85 percent. The census verifies no ARR band, so it cannot count this pool.
After the Deal-Volume Funnel (estimate): about 1 to 14 accounts at the 10 to 15 percent survival assumed for AI-native firms. The audit needs a cost export, not 500 closed deals, so the audit pool sits between the two figures.
Transition Share (not measured): the census frame was picked as usage or hybrid, so its 100 percent is selection. Zero of 16 example pages have a Wayback diff. ICONIQ's 57 percent is a state, not a transition.
SOM Implication (estimate, draft): 33 to 50 US Tier A accounts, 1.5M to 2.3M five-year wedge ARR, ACV unvalidated, awaiting founder sign-off. AI-native accounts sit inside Tier A: they reorder outreach and add none.
Seed ICP Factor (CFO estimate): a seed ICP shrinks that SOM about 3x in ARR, range 2x to 5x, to roughly 16 live accounts and 0.4M ARR, through lower ACV and higher churn.

5. Demand Signals to Log
Per Outreach Touch: section 3 rank, reply, interview booked. Below 5 percent replies after 150 touches (five days at 30 a day), fix the message or segment before judging any hypothesis.
Per Interview: margin stated with a number, the reconciliation artefact and its last run date, whether a cost export can arrive within a week, the answer to the 7,500 offer.
Hypothesis One Threshold: confirm if 8 or more of 15 founders state a margin number or name a customer group that costs more than it pays. Kill if fewer than 5 of 15 rank inference cost among their top three pricing problems.
Hypothesis Two Threshold: confirm if 6 or more of 15 name a manual reconciliation artefact and its last run date. Kill if fewer than 3 of 15 do.
Hypothesis Three Threshold: confirm on 2 audits paid at 7,500 from the first 5 qualified offers. If 3 of 5 decline on price, test 6,000 (F4). Kill if none of the first 8 qualified offers pays 6,000 or more.

6. What Would Change the Answer
Band Evidence: verified ARR for named AI-native accounts would turn the 13 to 92 estimate into a count.
Who Says Yes: Series B+ founders confirming while seed founders do not keeps the ICP. The reverse reopens F3.
Existing Tools: if founders already get per-customer cost from provider usage APIs, Orb or Metronome, hypothesis two dies whatever the pain.
Price Deflation: falling token prices shrink the cost problem. The durable problem is value-metric fit.
First Observed ACV: re-derive the SOM on the first paid audit that converts to a subscription, never before.
